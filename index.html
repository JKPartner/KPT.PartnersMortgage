This calculator is for illustration and education only. It is not a loan estimate, rate quote, approval or offer of credit. Results are estimates based on the numbers you enter and assume no new charges, on-time payments, and a rate that does not change. Each current debt is assumed to be paid at the monthly payment you enter until it is paid off. The 9% example rate is not an offer; actual rates, terms, loan amounts and closing costs depend on credit, income, home value, equity, property type and underwriting. Available equity is a rough estimate at 90% combined loan-to-value; some lenders allow up to 95%, and limits vary by lender, credit and property. HELOC rates are typically variable and can change, and many HELOCs begin with an interest-only draw period; this calculator assumes principal-and-interest payments. Closing costs, if any, can be added to the loan amount. A lower monthly payment over a longer term can increase the total interest you pay.
